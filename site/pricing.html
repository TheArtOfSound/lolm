Why these numbers (cheap + sustainable)

Free is a funnel, not free compute forever 10 runs + 3 visuals/day is enough to feel the product every day. Unlimited free would bankrupt the shared sandbox and browser-verify path.
Plus $7.99 undercuts $20 plans Cursor / Claude-class tools often land near $20. Plus is for real daily work without matching their IDE or frontier model bill of materials.
Daily caps = predictable cost Worst-case load is bounded. That lets us stay cheap without surprise overages or opaque token burn.
Self-host when you outgrow hosted Unlimited on your machine. Point lolm --base at localhost. BYOK pays your model provider only.

What am I buying?

1 run One hosted agent task (code, chat agent turn, research, operator). Multi-step write→run→fix inside that request still counts as one run.
1 visual build One browser-verified HTML/app/game. Separate because verify is expensive. Normal code runs do not spend visual quota.
Not sold: tokens No per-token LOLM fee on Free/Plus/Pro. Model cost is inside the quota (open cascade) or your BYOK invoice.
Self-host for unlimited Point the CLI or app at your own base URL. Hosted Free/Plus/Pro keep daily caps; local installs without admin enforcement are unlimited.

FAQ

Why did quotas get tighter than “unlimited feeling” numbers?

Earlier marketing-style caps (hundreds or thousands of free-ish runs) are not honest if the box pays for sandbox + browser verify. New caps are still generous for real use and keep Free/Plus sustainable.

Does the CLI share my web quota?

Yes when --base is the public API (default). Self-host with your own base URL is unlimited.

Can I upgrade mid-day?

Yes via Stripe on this page. Your license token unlocks the higher daily limits after claim.

What if I just need a few more runs today?

Upgrade to Plus or Pro on this page, wait for UTC midnight reset on Free, or self-host. One-time top-up packs are not live on this deployment yet.
