How it works

One agent.
No theatre.

LOLM is built for people who need code that runs and proof of what happened — not another chat that looks busy.

What you get

Multi-model raceOpening turns race GLM-class, gpt-oss-120b, and Llama 4 Maverick. Keep the candidate that compiles and runs.
Real sandboxWrite → run → fix in a network-isolated jail. Self-reported failures and broken checks block “shipped”.
Sealed receiptsEvery turn records files, commands, and control decisions in a hash-chained ledger you can audit.
NFET on codingAfter every run, LOLM measures latent dynamics of your code (or honest sandbox proxies) and can force verify, retrieve past receipts, branch to a new approach, or allow finalize — sealed into the receipt.

What NFET is

NFET is LOLM’s control layer. The five-stream graft (surface · latent SSM · regime · gate · memory) re-reads text and exposes entropy, hidden drift, gate balance, and regime entropy. A policy maps those to five actions: continue, retrieve, verify, branch, finalize. Chat has used this for a while. Coding now does too — so “shipped” is not model whim; it is measured dynamics plus a green sandbox.

What we deliberately hide

Lab dashboards live under More in the workspace. The product is the loop: describe a task, get working code, keep the receipt — with NFET decisions inside it.
